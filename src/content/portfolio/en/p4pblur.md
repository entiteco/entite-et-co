### Context
A Fine Arts graduate, P4PBLUR is an artist with a "creepycute" universe (a bittersweet blend of gothic and kawaii). Working mainly with acrylic markers, she wanted to bring her adorable monsters and strange characters to physical products for her community and her shop.

### Our approach
The main challenge was to preserve the spontaneity of her marker strokes and her colour nuances. We used digital textile printing to reproduce every detail of her artworks, complemented by a series of custom stickers featuring her signature characters.

### Deliverables
A merchandising range faithful to the artist's sensitive and quirky universe, taking her work beyond the canvas.
