### Context
Ready for the Fêtes de Bayonne? Team Kayou certainly was! We had the pleasure of kitting out this group of friends and athletes for their summer events. The goal was to create a unique, striking visual identity so the whole crew would stand out in the crowd.

### Our approach
For the best result on different garments, we combined two techniques:
- **On the black tank tops:** red and black flock for an ultra-sharp finish, with a premium velvet feel that makes all the difference.
- **On the white T-shirts:** digital printing to reproduce more complex, detailed and creative designs.

### Deliverables
The perfect combo to stand out during a race or a party. A durable, comfortable textile series for a memorable summer!
