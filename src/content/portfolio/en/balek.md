### Context
Balek Skateboard, an emerging streetwear and skate brand, wanted to develop an apparel range reflecting the urban spirit of its community. The challenge was to offer durable products, true to the brand's DNA and made with a local approach.

### Our approach
We opted for a production 100% printed and screen-printed in France. Screen printing was chosen to guarantee flawless durability over time, even under the demands and friction of skateboarding.

### Deliverables
A high-quality streetwear collection, ready for sale (Go Skate!), combining comfort and durability.
