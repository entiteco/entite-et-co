### Context

The French craft rum brand "La Yeyette au Rhum" (based in Pau) had a new look and exceptional, 100% natural recipes. It needed a new digital home and a visual identity worthy of its sunny world, to shine online and structure its growth.

### Our approach

Within the Entité & Co ecosystem, we led a global, cross-functional project:

- **Visual Identity & Branding (KVN HGS):** a striking new logo, eye-catching label designs so that every bottle stands out, plus communication materials and a social media strategy.
- **E-commerce Development:** a custom online store built on WordPress & WooCommerce to handle complex needs: consumer sales (with custom gift packs), services for events (weddings, birthdays), and a dedicated area for trade customers (bars, wine shops, resellers).

### Deliverables

A smooth, user-friendly and responsive e-commerce platform, paired with a strong, assertive brand identity, ready to take customers' taste buds on a journey while simplifying the brand's day-to-day management.
