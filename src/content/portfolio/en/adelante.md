### Context
The Adelante Polo Team wanted a complete range of apparel and accessories in its colours. The goal was to combine the elegance of the polo world with comfortable, high-performance materials suited to matches, training and life around the field.

### Our approach
We selected textiles designed for sport and applied different marking techniques depending on the item: precision embroidery on polo shirts and towels, for durability and prestige, and high-quality printing on hoodies and neck warmers (snoods).

### Deliverables
A complete pack of premium sports gear (polo shirts, hoodies, neck warmers, towels) reinforcing the visual identity of the Adelante Polo Team, on and off the field.
