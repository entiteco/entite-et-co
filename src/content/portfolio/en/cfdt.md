### Context

For its gatherings and professional events, the CFDT Enedis union branch needed physical communication items that were useful and environmentally friendly, to replace disposable cups.

### Our approach

We selected "Ecocup" reusable cups (translucent 33cl format). The design was printed in four-colour process to strictly follow the union's brand guidelines and official colours, with a print proof approved beforehand.

### Deliverables

A series of custom, sturdy and eco-friendly cups, perfect for spreading the brand image at physical events.
