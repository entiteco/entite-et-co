### Context

A custom home builder for more than 18 years (first in Gradignan, now based in Villenave d'Ornon), "Pavillons de l'Atlantique" helps around thirty families a year with their life project. The goal was to modernise its online presence to reflect its reliability, its strict guarantees (CCMI build contract, ten-year warranty, structural damage insurance) and its local roots.

### Our approach

We designed a showcase website focused on clarity, trust and user experience:

- **Usability & Design:** a modern, clean and fully responsive interface showcasing their know-how and end-to-end support.
- **Information Architecture:** clear structure for the builder's guarantees and emphasis on the company's human, local dimension.
- **SEO & Performance:** technical best practices to maximise local visibility among home-building projects in Gironde.

### Deliverables

A professional, highly secure and optimised showcase website, serving as a trusted acquisition channel for future homeowners.
